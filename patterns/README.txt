Lithologic pattern tiles (601.png ... 733.png): black pattern on a transparent background, 1200 dpi.
Patterns of the FGDC Digital Cartographic Standard for Geologic Map Symbolization,
FGDC-STD-013-2006, Appendix A, chapter 37 (US Federal Geographic Data Committee).
Tiles from https://github.com/davenquinn/geologic-patterns (CC0 1.0, public domain);
718 cut from the FGDC standard. Not covered by the GPL - see the separate section in ../LICENSE.
