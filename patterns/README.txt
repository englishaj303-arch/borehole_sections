Lithologic pattern tiles (601.png ... 733.png): pattern on a transparent background, 1200 dpi (black; the plugin recolours the lines pale cream on dark fills).
Patterns of the FGDC Digital Cartographic Standard for Geologic Map Symbolization,
FGDC-STD-013-2006, Appendix A, chapter 37 (US Federal Geographic Data Committee).
Tiles from https://github.com/davenquinn/geologic-patterns (CC0 1.0, public domain);
718 cut from the FGDC standard. Not covered by the GPL - see the separate section in ../LICENSE.

901.png (soil / humus) and 902.png (dirt) are not in the FGDC standard: they were drawn for this plugin
(script make_soil_dirt_tiles.py in the source repository, GPL like the code; it is not part of the plugin package). Like all the tiles they are black lines on a transparent background; the plugin
makes the pale cream variant itself for dark colours of the lithology (see patterns.line_is_light).

903.png-909.png (fill, rubble, organic clay, weathered rock, eluvium, colluvium / deluvium, alluvium) are not in the FGDC
standard either: they were added by the author of the plugin (black lines on a transparent background).
